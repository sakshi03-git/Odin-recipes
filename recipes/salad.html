Lasagna Recipe Home

Description

Tis is a classic, rich, and cheesy homemade lasagna made with layers of pasta, savory meat sauce, and creamy cheese.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef or sausage
  • Ricotta and Mozzarella cheese
  • Marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles according to the oackage instructions.
  2. Cook the ground meat in a skillet and stir in the marinara sauce.
  3. Layer noodles, saucce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  4. Bake at 375 F (190 C) for 30 minutes until bubbling.
